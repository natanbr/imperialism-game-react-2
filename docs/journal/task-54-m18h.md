# Task 54 — Town freight and receipts

Size M. On `claude/m18h-towns`, T-03 covers FR-621, FR-622 and the freight and planning parts of FR-625. The five defaults retain the status **inferred, owner to confirm**. [Build split and defaults](../specs/m18h-towns.md#build-split)

The logistics phase ships tile resources, Fish and the owner's mature town output against a single car capacity. It honours explicit commodity targets first, including zero. Automatic freight follows ordinary collection order, then each province's bundle in chain order. When towns share a commodity, the first offering province gets the first delivered units. Stock receives the shipment once. Gold and Gems still pay cash. Each producing province gets a dated receipt, even when it delivers nothing.

Transport, the Armoury's horse estimate and domestic planning use the same freight rules. A computer planning its last transport Order can account for a new connection while its town resource count uses only the deposits known when it planned. News checks the receipt's original recipient before grouping lines. T-04 takes the resulting figures into map, inspection and Capital views.

Objection raised during review — adding two towns' lumber before scheduling their automatic cars displaced the first town's furniture. The six-car regression distinguishes the two outcomes. It now ships one grain, one Fish, one fabric, two lumber and one furniture. The later town records zero. An explicit lumber target of three instead draws from both towns.
