# M18g — The original game's information screens

**Design base `9d2f9eb` · Stats verified in Task 41. Books and diplomatic map modes planned for Task 42.** A player can compare the Great Powers across turns, look up the market for a commodity they bid on, inspect their garrisons, and read their country's diplomatic map. These are the information screens from RULES-GAP-22 that the remake can support with its present rules. The ledger at the design base ends at FR-609 and NR-039. Parked M18d owns FR-580–587. ## What supplies each screen

**Rankings.** The original gives category names and relative bars. These **inferred** remake measures use settled data. Within a tie show the same place and bar, list names in founding order, and skip the shared places for the next rank (1, 1, 3). The historic view plots place by turn. Neither chart path lengths nor accessibility text encode the private margin between two powers.

| Label in Stats | Quantity used only to establish place at settlement |
|---|---|
| Council standing | Existing `councilStanding` for powers holding a province. Omit this series on a world without provinces. The Council still chooses its nominees and counts votes with its own rules. |
| Army strength (land) | Existing `militaryStrength`, the same comparison Diplomacy uses. On a world without armies or provinces, omit the land series. The label distinguishes it from the original combined army and navy measure. |
| Industry (installed capacity) | `scoreOf(...).capacity`, the productive capacity already used by Diplomacy. |
| Labour (available output) | `labourTotal(industry)`, which accounts for grade, sickness and training. |
| Transport (freight cars) | Cars in service from `nation.transportCapacity` at the end of the resolved turn. |
| Merchant marine (cargo capacity) | `cargoHolds(nation.merchantMarine)`, including a legacy holds-only fleet. |
| World exports (units sold this turn) | Sum the resolved turn's ordinary `goods-sold.amount` per Great Power. Zero sales is a valid tie. Buyer events, forced sales and receipts in dollars contribute nothing. |

**Recording boundary.** `GameState` holds the current position. The store keeps 20 `TurnReport` entries with events and optional treasury figures. Neither holds a complete chart series. The order log is a replay input. The Stats page must not rebuild a campaign from it. After `advanceTurn` returns from `resolveTurn`, use the returned state and events to derive the ranks, and save them in the same store update as the corresponding report. Index by `TurnReport.turn` (the turn just played). The first sample is the first settlement. Before it, explain when the chart starts. A loaded game with no series starts at its next settlement. Gaps stay gaps. Drawing or restoring the view cannot append a sample. Carry the optional field through session serialization and restore. A missing or unusable display record costs only its history. No history is passed to a planner, validator, resolver, score, Council, victory check or replay function as a decision input. [Store and turn report](../../src/app/store/gameSlice.ts) · [Save envelope](../../src/app/store/persistence.ts) · [Replay](../../src/app/store/sessionReplay.ts) · [Current Stats](../../src/app/components/HUD/StatsDialog.tsx)

**Trade Book.** Put a book entry in the partners Board alongside Board, Details and Trade policies. Keep the FR-575 Details content and its owner's placement question intact. For a current page, read the viewer's bid, sellers and restrictions from `boardOfTrade(game, viewerId)`. Show the seller's Great Power preference order from `partnerRanking(diplomacyAfterThisTurn(game, viewerId), minorId)` with the label “Seller preference”. This order ranks powers independently of who has bid. Do not infer which flags the original suppressed (H4). For the settled page, check the viewer's positive bid in `lastTradeOrdersOf` **before** reading public participants. Use that same round's archive for Great Power offer quantities and bidder names. Join only matching, viewer-addressed bought or missed minor entries from the dated Deal Book report. Label these as our encounters. The engine does not archive a complete previous minor offer book, its preferences, or other bidders' free holds. Never substitute a current figure. A missing round has no selectable past page. Two islands retains its anonymous world market. The Deal Book continues to explain actual trades and reconcile the treasury. [Board queries](../../src/app/engine/market/partners.ts) · [FR-575 Details](../../src/app/components/market/BoardDetails.tsx) · [Deal Book](../../src/app/engine/market/dealBook.ts)

**Garrisons and maps.** Scope the province picker to the active nation and read `armyAt(game, viewerId, provinceId)` after checking ownership. Reuse the Army's Force Size, health and status wording, including pending orders visible to their issuer. Read the diplomatic overlays from settled `relationOf`, `relationLevel`, `consulates`, `embassies`, `pact`, `alliance`, `war` and `minor.colonyOf`. Membership in an office array identifies the **sender**. Map fills follow current ownership. The list can describe a colony whose land now belongs to the conqueror. Use the existing mode by default, keep sea plain and the home country identified, and give text for every tint or icon. The information modes have no policy editor. On the world-market route there is no FR-579 tab. ## Other screens in RULES-GAP-22

| Screen or feature | Reason for leaving it out |
|---|---|
| Offer Sheet and Foreign Minister recommendations or automatic purchases | Accept, reject, quantity and “No more offers” interrupt a trade and spend cash and holds. The remake settles at End Turn. Recommendations need a decision policy. The existing diplomatic Accept Offer dialog answers peace or alliance proposals. |
| Foreign Scouting Report | The original estimates and sometimes leaves unit types unknown. Adjacency, observers and identification accuracy would need reconnaissance rules. Preserve the existing Attack preview. |
| Task Force Report orders and defender deployment prompt | Patrol, blockade, landing, movement and choosing who deploys troops are commands. Existing naval and Army orders already have their own screens. Merchant-interception reports also require an interception event the remake does not generate. |
| Garrison upgrades and disband shortcuts | An upgrade spends resources and changes a regiment. Disbanding already issues an Order from Army. Neither belongs in the book. |
| Reward and advice popups | Generals, medals, statues, minister recommendations and danger alerts need award or trigger rules. The current news and battle results report events that already occurred. |
| Technology, University, Shipyard | The audit predates their Capital screens (FR-511, FR-514, FR-526, FR-548). Buying, recruiting and commissioning change state. Expanding their catalogs is separate work. |
| Interior and Diplomatic Strength composites, full military projection, and original score weights | The original combinations and weights lack an established equivalent here (E1, H13). Use the named component rankings above. Original Military includes naval power. The existing Council ballot, Stats score and Game over ranking already have working counterparts. |

## Open questions and inferred defaults

| Decision | Build default and what would change it |
|---|---|
| How closely should the new charts match the original measures? | **Accepted for Task 41.** Show the seven named measures, rank-scaled bars and one record after every settlement. Keep gaps for earlier turns in older saves. Original screenshots establish a snapshot. The original history interval and hidden weights are unknown. |
| **[NEEDS CLARIFICATION] Should the Trade Book eventually replace Details?** | **Inferred.** Keep both for now. The book groups bid-specific markets by round. Details still explains potential trades before the player bids. Moving that information would resolve the existing FR-575 owner decision. |
| **[NEEDS CLARIFICATION] Whose diplomatic map can the player inspect?** | **Inferred.** Fix the reference country to the human's nation. Selecting a foreign country shows the pair's settled relationship and our offices there. A later foreign-perspective view would need a field-by-field account of what an outside observer can see. |
