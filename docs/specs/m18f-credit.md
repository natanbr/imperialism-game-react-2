# M18f — Credit, the Deal Book and goods without a source

**Design base `21ea18b` · status planned.** A player can authorize credit, see what a purchase would leave to spend, and account for every dollar of interest or forced sales after End Turn. The goods lists explain how stock can reach the player on the map actually being played. Parked M18d owns FR-580–587. M18e ended at FR-598 and NR-038.

## What the rules establish

Quotations below are from the repository's Imperialism I rules digest. Its manual page markers and screenshot tags identify the underlying evidence.

**Current remake.** `engine/world/startWorld.ts` writes optional `debt: 0` and `creditLimit: 5000`. Neither field authorizes spending. Civilian jobs, bids, training, technology, recruitment and diplomacy reserve cash through their Orders. Partner settlement uses reserved bid cash and available treasury for a price premium. Trade precedes ordered disbands, upkeep and production. FR-202 removes the newest unpaid regiments before charging cash. The upkeep forecast simulates standing grants and bid premiums. The Deal Book reads a turn's events and reported treasuries. Its Treasury table itemizes upkeep, diplomacy and recruiting, with remaining movements under Other. `HUD/NationStats.tsx` renders the live cash inside TopBar. ## Settlement details for implementation

1. **Open, then spend.** Validate the owning controller in the engine. A successful `openCredit` initializes an optional account on that nation. A repeated request is idempotent. The authorized facility persists. The opening action neither draws principal nor posts a cash receipt. Keep one signed treasury. Derive debt as `max(0, -treasury)` and buying power as `max(0, treasury + limit)`. The old placeholder fields alone never identify an account. With no opening Order the existing replay reconstructs exactly the old position, including a save made halfway through an order sequence.
2. **Reserve at Order time.** Apply the usual net cost of an edit after releasing its own refundable reservation. Cancellations keep the existing refund timing. Only increases in dollar outlay require buying power. Cash-only embassies and repeated grants use signed cash. A newly ordered grant and a consulate follow the ordinary credit check. A successfully reserved bid can still settle from its reserve. A premium can use the balance left under the line, and units beyond it pass on. Respect holds, resources and diplomatic barriers in both cash and credit cases. Apply a refusal atomically.
3. **Close the trade phase.** Collect the open nation's ordinary `goods-sold.income` once per actual sale in either mode, at the amount received after any subsidy. Post its income-based limit with this turn included. Run voluntary disbands as usual. For an open human account, charge the normal full upkeep even when it causes an excess. In the finance step immediately following upkeep, assess interest on the resulting debt. If it was positive, record its balance, basis, per-turn rate and rounded amount, even if rounding gives $0. Later logistics proceeds count when actually received. Phase effects emit events through the turn context. They create no synthetic player Orders.
4. **Sell if necessary.** Use the refreshed limit throughout interest and liquidation. Process tradables in `BOARD_ORDER` at the post-trade posted prices. Sell the lesser of on-hand units and the whole units required to cover the remaining excess. Physical stock includes unsold offers already returned. It excludes undelivered imports, assets already committed and removed by Orders, raw food, and precious metals. **Inferred** B10 choice: a standing factory instruction does not physically reserve its inputs. Seized inputs can reduce this turn's output under the existing production rule. Show the item sold in the statement. A forced sale consumes no holds and creates no demand, diplomacy gain or qualifying income. Recheck the spending block against current debt after each receipt or refund. End Turn, zero-cash production, transport, voluntary disband and recovery remain possible even with nothing saleable.
5. **Reconcile both views of cash.** Signed closing cash equals opening cash plus ordinary sales minus purchases, upkeep and interest, plus forced-sale proceeds and the other signed movements already reported. Give the finance entries their own lines and remove them from the existing Other residual. Debt, limit and buying power describe a balance and never count towards the change. Use a settlement event or optional report figures for historical debt, rate and limit, and actual end-of-turn treasury for closing debt after any later receipts. Keep FR-396's trade-only net. The live account panel can differ from the dated statement after a new Order. Forecasts use only the nation's public view of potential deals. Redact rival credit and private Orders just as the market forecast already redacts rival cash.

**Worked settlement (test inputs).** At a posted limit of $922 and pre-interest cash of −$625, the **inferred** high tier produces $38 after rounding and cash closes at −$663. The original screen shows those dollars but labels the rate 12%. The remake would label the charge **6% per turn (inferred)**. At the same limit exactly $461 of debt takes the lower tier. On crossing the limit, a single unit can cover more than the excess. Credit its full sale price and stop as soon as debt is within the limit.
