# M18h — Town development

Task 51 · Spec only · 2026-11-25 · `claude/m18h-towns` · Design base `0b98682` (M18d merged in PR #54)

## Rules summary

A province's town stays idle until a connected depot or port is on its seat or a neighbouring hex. It then supplies materials from the resources of the whole province and later consumer goods, up to half the materials. A town keeps producing alongside the province's raw output. The owner supplies freight cars to bring that output to the warehouse. The manual's worked example uses a forest province and the Capital's furniture factory. [Transport §§4–6, manual pp. 48, 53–54 as summarized](../rules/04-transport.md)

**Inferred from player observations [W12].** Every number in the following table is an inferred threshold or yield from the wiki. The manual illustrates the wood chain but gives no complete schedule. Read each commodity column independently. Resource counts are minima in tiles, capacity is installed size, and output is units **offered per settled turn before freight**.

| Known cotton + wool tiles | Known timber tiles | Known coal tiles **and** known iron ore tiles | Required Capital capacity for the chain | Fabric + clothing, lumber + furniture, or steel + hardware |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 2 | 2 of each | 4 | 1 material + 0 goods |
| 4 | 4 | 4 of each | 8 | 2 materials + 1 good |
| 6 | 6 | 6 of each | 12 | 3 materials + 1 good |
| 8 | 8 | 8 of each | 16 | 4 materials + 2 goods |

**Factory default (inferred).** Use Clothing Factory (`clothingFactory`) for fibres, Furniture Factory (`furnitureFactory`) for timber and Metal Works (`metalWorks`) for coal and iron. The table in the digest names the textile, lumber and steel *mills*. Its accompanying note describes the final-product factories, and the manual's wood example names the furniture factory. The choice affects the first material as well as later goods. Paper, canned food, fuel and armaments lie outside these chains. The reported oil-to-fuel exception and the Improved Railyard reward are separate from the manual's town output. [Transport §§5–6](../rules/04-transport.md) · [Unresolved evidence, B1](../rules/gaps.md)

**Timing default (inferred).** [W12] reports first output after six connected turns and later steps about two turns after expansion. Count the first settlement with a usable hub as connected turn one. On the sixth such settlement, pay the first material tier if the chain qualifies. If the resources or first factory qualify later, start on that later settlement. After first output, require two further connected settlements meeting the next tier for each increase, even if several tiers are already supported. Count a completed expansion at that turn's connectivity check. Ignore a queued expansion. Retain earned progress through a route closure. If support drops, retain matured tiers, limit current output to the supported tier and restart any unfinished upgrade interval whose prerequisite was lost. Never stockpile missed production. [Player-derived timing and manual trigger](../rules/04-transport.md#6-town-development-free-materials-and-goods)

## What the player may know

| Viewer | Town facts available under FR-497 |
|---|---|
| Human inspecting their own nation | The seat, ordinary visible resources (including discovered mineral tiles), installed capacity, active route, elapsed progress and next supported tier. Label the current estimate separately from the previous turn's dated receipts. The previous owner can still read a receipt addressed to them for an earlier turn. |
| Human inspecting a foreign province | The visible location, province name, current owner and existing public map features. The new town marker on foreign land gives no development stage. A hidden deposit cannot affect an apparent resource count, a progress bar, a tooltip, a News line or an accessible name. |
| Scripted or computer player | The same owner-scoped facts and previous public information as its human counterpart. Existing stock and domestic freight can affect its ordinary Orders. Resolving income for all owners gives the planner no access to another owner's private input. Normal and Demo use this contract for all M18h surfaces. FR-562's existing Demo dashboard exception is for the human only. |

## Where the rule fits

- **Town and owner.** Use `Province.seatTileId` for the town and `tileIds` for the resource census. Fixed-continent and generated seats can lie on ordinary resource terrain. Two islands has no provinces. Compare the province with `capitalProvinceOf` for its founding country, using `startOwnerOf` for old saves. A conquered founding minor capital stays excluded on subsequent transfers. A founding Great Power capital is idle again if its original owner recovers it. Do not count a closed port's rail-freight flag as an alternative depot. Advance progress once, then give its offered amounts to logistics. This turn's factory jobs have already run. ## Open questions and inferred defaults

The orchestrator accepted all five defaults below for the M18h build. Their status is **inferred, owner to confirm**.

| Decision for the owner | Inferred default for the build |
|---|---|
| Which Capital building unlocks a chain, and how far does it grow? | Use the matching final-goods factory for all tiers, following the manual's wood example. Stop at the last documented tier, installed capacity 16. The digest also supports reading the table as mill-gated and notes that the pattern may continue. An original-game comparison with different mill and factory sizes would resolve both. |
| Which resource tiles count, and can a mixed province develop several chains? | Count known resource-bearing tiles once, including unconnected or unimproved tiles and both kinds of timber. Count mineral types only once revealed. Run each qualifying chain independently. An original mixed-province turn log could establish whether one specialty takes priority. |
| How does the growth clock behave across expansion, interruption and capture? | Use the inferred six connected settlements to start and two more per upgrade. Pause through disconnection. Reset on owner change, waive only the startup delay for an eligible captured Great Power capital, and permanently exclude founding minor capitals. [W12] describes the exceptions but gives no rule for inherited progress. |
| Which town loses freight when cars run short? | Honour existing commodity allocations, then ordinary collection order, then town offers in stable province order. Discard the unshipped balance each turn. Gap B1 leaves shortage behavior open. |
| Does an oil town also supply fuel? | Keep fuel refinery-only for domestic production. The manual's shippable list excludes it, while [W14/W15] describe a possible exception. Adding it would need a supported oil table and a separate balance comparison. |
