# M18h — Town development

Task 51 · Spec only · 2026-11-25 · `claude/m18h-towns` · Design base `0b98682` (M18d merged in PR #54)

## Rules summary

A province's town stays idle until a connected depot or port is on its seat or a neighbouring hex. It then supplies materials from the resources of the whole province and later consumer goods, up to half the materials. A town keeps producing alongside the province's raw output. The owner supplies freight cars to bring that output to the warehouse. The manual's worked example uses a forest province and the Capital's furniture factory. [Transport §§4–6, manual pp. 48, 53–54 as summarized](../rules/04-transport.md)

**Inferred from player observations [W12].** Every number in the following table is an inferred threshold or yield from the wiki. The manual illustrates the wood chain but gives no complete schedule. Read each commodity column independently. Resource counts are minima in tiles, capacity is installed size, and output is units **offered per settled turn before freight**.

| Known cotton + wool tiles | Known timber tiles | Known coal tiles **and** known iron ore tiles | Required Capital capacity for the chain | Fabric + clothing, lumber + furniture, or steel + hardware |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 2 | 2 of each | 4 | 1 material + 0 goods |
| 4 | 4 | 4 of each | 8 | 2 materials + 1 good |
| 6 | 6 | 6 of each | 12 | 3 materials + 1 good |
| 8 | 8 | 8 of each | 16 | 4 materials + 2 goods |

**Factory default (inferred).** Use Clothing Factory (`clothingFactory`) for fibres, Furniture Factory (`furnitureFactory`) for timber and Metal Works (`metalWorks`) for coal and iron. The table in the digest names the textile, lumber and steel *mills*. Its accompanying note describes the final-product factories, and the manual's wood example names the furniture factory. The choice affects the first material as well as later goods. Paper, canned food, fuel and armaments lie outside these chains. The reported oil-to-fuel exception and the Improved Railyard reward are separate from the manual's town output. [Transport §§5–6](../rules/04-transport.md) · [Unresolved evidence, B1](../rules/gaps.md)

**Timing default (inferred).** [W12] reports first output after six connected turns and later steps about two turns after expansion. Count the first settlement with a usable hub as connected turn one. On the sixth such settlement, pay the first material tier if the chain qualifies. If the resources or first factory qualify later, start on that later settlement. After first output, require two further connected settlements meeting the next tier for each increase, even if several tiers are already supported. Count a completed expansion at that turn's connectivity check. Ignore a queued expansion. Retain earned progress through a route closure. If support drops, retain matured tiers, limit current output to the supported tier and restart any unfinished upgrade interval whose prerequisite was lost. Never stockpile missed production. [Player-derived timing and manual trigger](../rules/04-transport.md#6-town-development-free-materials-and-goods)
