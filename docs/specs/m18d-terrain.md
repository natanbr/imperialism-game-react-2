## Starting maps, freight and art

The continent and two-islands are hand-made maps. Preserve the coastline, capitals, province boundaries, existing starting stocks and existing authored mineral deposits. Add terrain to the mirrored land template and supply mountain prospecting opportunities on minor-origin land on the continent. Put rivers on contiguous land reaching a sea mouth. A river does not give the entire inland watercourse the collection radius of a port. Fish uses ordinary logistics. A port on a hill or mountain is unavailable to human Engineers under rules 04. River connectivity must reflect province transfers and existing blockade effects.

Warehouse lists sourced goods at their true amounts. Transport shows the ordinary collection and allocation of Wool, Fish, Oil and Horses. Fuel is made in the capital and has no freight row. On the Board, Wool, Horses, Oil and Fuel use ordinary trade rules. Raw Fish is food and has no Board row. Gold and Gems go directly to cash and have neither a Warehouse nor Board row. Flag domestic source absence where the scenario offers no source (including older hand-made maps), retaining usable inventory and trades. A locked well or refinery is a potential source after Oil Drilling. Do not present a mineral as discovered to explain a good's availability.

Use the existing `public/assets/ink-and-wash/terrain/` pictures for all fourteen terrains, including `mountains.png`, `fertile-hills.png`, `wool-pasture.png`, `scrub-forest.png`, `horse-ranch.png` and the desert and tundra bases. `goods/` covers all thirteen raw goods and Fuel. `improvements/` covers Wool and the wells, mines and ranches. The pack has no dedicated river overlay or empty-survey picture. Art request: a transparent ink-and-wash river overlay and a small empty-survey mark; use the map's river symbol/ink token and a simple empty-survey indication pending final art. The bridge uses FR-570's timber trestle.

## Inferred defaults and open questions

- **Survey odds and deposits (inferred).** Which distribution matches the original? Give empty and each eligible mineral equal weight on unassigned terrain; one draw per completed unassigned survey, retaining authored deposits. The rules describe some double deposits. The engine's one-resource model represents one outcome per tile. Confirm original weights, oil failures and the frequency of double finds.
- **Gems (inferred).** Rules 02 records conflicting passages. Use original minor-nation territory for generated Gems, including after conquest. Confirm whether Great Power mountains can also hold Gems.
- **River direction (inferred).** The map records land hexes with `hasRiver`, without elevations or directed channels. Use a contiguous owned river route to an adjacent sea mouth. Confirm how the original resolves branches or several mouths. A river bridge requires Iron Railroad Bridge in addition to the land permission; rules 04 prices swamp track but gives no separate crossing surcharge. Use the existing terrain price.
- **Fishing limits (inferred).** Count each adjacent fishing hex once per nation, even when covered by several ports. Use the normal land ownership rule on inland river fish; adjacent sea is unowned. Confirm overlapping fisheries and foreign river banks.
- **Fixed yields.** Horse output of one per turn is observed in rules 02. Keep existing inferred job prices and durations (`gaps.md` A4); no new balance prices. A global rollout of all historical development gates needs a separate resolution of FR-523/528.
