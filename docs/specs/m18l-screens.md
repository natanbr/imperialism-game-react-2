**Design base `fcb1553` (main after M18j) plus the M18k play-test fixes. ## Open questions and inferred defaults (owner to confirm)

1. **Exact or fogged report.** Built exact, because Q32 makes defenders fully visible. The original shows "units of unknown type" and a General improves accuracy; that needs a fog rule for the Attack preview and the computer too.
2. **Who can scout:** a bordering owned province with a regiment, a bordering army, or a ship in a touching sea zone (inferred). Built (T-01): the remake has no named sea zones (card Q144 chose province approaches) and every continent coast lies on one connected sea, so a whole-sea rule would let any squadron scout every coast. A squadron of ours counts when it lies off the province itself (its approach), and the estimator reads "Estimated by our fleet off [province]". A squadron at its home berth or off another province does not count. Among several bordering provinces the first in province order names the estimator.
3. **Military words:** ratio thresholds 0.5 / 0.8 / 1.25 / 2 against our own strength (inferred; the original's scale is unknown).
4. **Which strength Military compares** (M18l review). Built as the spec says: the FR-226 defence of the provinces a country owns, militia and fort protection included, as the Attack preview counts it. Stats → Rankings' army placing (FR-610) uses FR-204 strength without militia or protection, so two Great Powers tied in Stats can read differently here: the one whose regiments sit behind forts or include militia reads higher. The mode note now says so. Using `militaryStrength` instead would make the two screens agree and rate every militia-only minor Poor.
5. **Singular regiment names** (M18l review). The report reads "1 Regulars" and "1 Minutemen", as the battle report ("1 Regulars, 1 Artillery", FR-277) and the Treasury and news lines ("1 Regulars recruited") do; the original reads "1 Demolitionist". A singular name for a count of 1 would be one change across those screens, not this report alone.
